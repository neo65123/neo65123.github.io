Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy describes how MyFaithTeacher-auto ("the app") handles data. The app is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel MyFaithTeacher and is used only with the owner's own Google account.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • An OAuth access token for the owner's Google account, used to upload videos, thumbnails and metadata to the owner's channel, to add them to the owner's own playlists, and to post comments on the owner's own videos.
  • Public information about YouTube videos and channels returned by YouTube search (such as titles, view counts and subscriber counts), used only to choose topics for new videos.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • All data is stored only on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers or any other YouTube users.
  • Public search data is kept only as long as needed to choose a topic and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • The app does not use cookies or other tracking technologies.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page. Deleting the app's folder on the owner's computer removes all stored data. To request deletion or ask questions, contact neo65123@gmail.com.
