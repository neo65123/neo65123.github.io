MyFaithTeacher

MyFaithTeacher is a YouTube channel with calm, documentary-style videos that retell the great stories and people of the Bible from beginning to end, with historical and archaeological background woven in.

Scripture quotations come from the World English Bible (public domain). Where Christian traditions disagree, the videos present the main views fairly instead of choosing one side.

MyFaithTeacher-auto

MyFaithTeacher-auto is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • search public YouTube videos to find which Bible topics viewers are interested in,
  • upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's channel,
  • post a comment on the channel's own videos inviting viewers to share their thoughts.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
